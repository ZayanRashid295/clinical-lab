\chapter*{Abstract}
\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

Medical students need more than question banks: they must practice interviews, clinical reasoning, and documentation under feedback aligned with OSCE standards, while institutions require visibility into cohort progress. This thesis presents \textbf{MedPrepAI}, an intelligent platform for virtual consultation, medical training, and clinical workflow, implemented in the \emph{clinical-lab} repository.

MedPrepAI unifies four Gemini-powered modes (Practice, Learning, Evaluation, Shadow) with a full learning management system, faculty dashboards, assignments, Stripe subscriptions, chat, notifications, and study tools. A NestJS API and Next.js frontend use Prisma on MySQL with JWT, RBAC, and feature entitlements. Encounter data---messages, diagnoses, SOAP notes, and hint usage---are persisted for analytics and grading.

We describe the architecture, database design, LLM orchestration, and security model, and evaluate the system through scenario-based case studies. Results show that functional, pedagogical, institutional, and commercial requirements are met in an integrated, deployable codebase suitable for medical education.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Medical education; virtual patient; large language models; OSCE; learning management system; clinical workflow.
